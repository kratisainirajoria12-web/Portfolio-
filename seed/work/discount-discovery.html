Know What the App Does

Discount Discovery App is a Shopify app that lets merchants create almost any kind of discount. No coding needed. Set up BOGO deals, bundles, tiered pricing, or volume discounts in just a few clicks, and apply them automatically at checkout. Merchants can even offer different discounts to different countries, or fine-tune deals based on what's actually in a customer's cart. It's designed to help brands boost sales and repeat purchases without added complexity.

Beyond the Wizard: One - Click Discount Playbooks

Not every merchant wants to configure a discount from scratch, some just want a proven strategy they can turn on. So, alongside the guided setup flow, a library of one-click playbooks: pre-built discounts, already configured around specific goals like increasing AOV, boosting conversion, or driving first purchase, and grouped into categories so merchants can find the right strategy. Each playbook comes with its conditions already set, a merchant just picks one, sets eligibility, and it's live.

*Some playbooks appearing under more than one goal depending on how a merchant wants to use them.

One Guided Flow

Every discount, regardless of type, answers four key questions: what it is, how it works, who qualifies, and if it's ready to launch. So, I created a single streamlined setup: Type → Method → Eligibility → Additional Settings → Summary.

Built for Real Use

The playbooks themselves are built around discount strategies ecommerce brands actually run. So, these are mapped to a specific merchant goal: Increase AOV, Increase Conversion and Increase Purchase.

The Impact

The app changed what it actually takes for a merchant to launch a discount. Merchants can now easily step-up a discount using simple steps.
That matters most for smaller merchants without a dedicated growth or marketing person, the app is doing the strategic thinking that a merchant might otherwise skip because it felt too complicated to figure out.
