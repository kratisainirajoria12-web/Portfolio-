About

I came for the visuals, stayed for the why.

I started out in visual design, and slowly got more curious about what happens after something looks good — whether people understand it, trust it, and actually use it. Three years in, that curiosity is the job.

Today I work across product design, CRO, e-commerce and branding, usually somewhere between a spreadsheet of session recordings and a very full Figma file.

Design approach

Like clay: rough first, then shaped until it feels right.

Continue

  1. 01

    Research

    Interviews, analytics, session recordings and a look at what competitors get wrong. I collect more than I need, on purpose.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Sticky notes, affinity maps, journey maps. The goal is one clear problem statement everyone on the team agrees with.

  3. 03

    Explore

    Lots of rough options, fast. Sketches and low-fi flows so it’s cheap to be wrong and easy to change direction.

  4. 04

    Design

    Committing to a direction: grids, components, real content and every edge case — empty, error, loading.

  5. 05

    Refine

    Usability tests, A/B tests and polish. Shipping is the midpoint; the numbers after launch decide the next round.
